About Show Shuffler

Ever spent longer scrolling for something to watch than actually watching it? That's the whole reason this exists.

Show Shuffler is a simple idea: add the shows you love, hit one button, and let it land you on a random episode — then take you straight there. No more staring at menus, no more "idk, what do you wanna watch." Just press shuffle and go.

How it works

You build a list of your shows. When you shuffle, the site picks a random episode you haven't seen recently and points you to where you can watch it — your streaming apps, or right here on the site when a network has posted the full episode officially and for free on YouTube. It even queues the next one automatically.

The honest part

Show Shuffler doesn't host any video. It's a discovery tool that links you to legal sources — the same way a guide points you to a restaurant without cooking the meal. Everything you watch comes from the services and official channels that own it.

Who built it

Show Shuffler is an independent project built by one person who was tired of the endless scroll. It's free, and it's growing. If you have ideas or run into something broken, I'd genuinely love to hear it: hello@showshuffle.live.
